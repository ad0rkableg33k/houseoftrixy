A little cardboard chaos, done right.

House of Trixy began when I wandered back into collecting Pokémon cards and remembered just how much I love having a hobby — the thrill of opening a pack, finding an old favorite, and chasing something special.

Somewhere along the way, my collection grew into more cards than any reasonable person could keep, so I turned the happy excess into a small business. Everything here has been handled with care and listed honestly.

⚠ We ship to the Continental USA only. Orders from outside this area will be cancelled.
